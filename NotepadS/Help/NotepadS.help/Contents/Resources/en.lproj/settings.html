NotepadS Help

Settings

Choose NotepadS › Settings… (⌘,).

SettingEffect
Font, SizeThe editor font. All open windows change at once.
Tab widthHow many spaces wide a tab is.
Insert spaces when pressing TabTab inserts spaces up to the next tab stop instead of a tab character.
Keep indentation on new linesReturn keeps the indentation of the current line.
Wrap lines (new windows)Whether new windows wrap long lines. View › Wrap Lines changes it for one window.

Restore Defaults… puts every setting back to how it was when NotepadS was new: font, indentation, wrapping, the minimap, invisible characters, the tab bar, Open and Find panel options, and window and panel positions. Your documents and your own languages stay. Open windows keep their current wrapping, minimap and invisible characters until you close them; the font changes at once.
