Keyboard shortcuts

The most useful shortcuts in NotepadS.

ShortcutAction
⌘NNew document (in a new tab)
⌘OOpen
⌘SSave
⌘PPrint (also to PDF)
⌘F, ⌘G, ⇧⌘GFind, find next, find previous
⌥⌘FFind and replace (find bar)
⇧⌥⌘FFind and replace with regular expressions
⌘LGo to Line
⌃⌘WWrap Lines on or off
⌥⌘IShow Invisibles
⌘=, ⌘−, ⌘0Bigger, smaller, default font size
⌘,Settings
