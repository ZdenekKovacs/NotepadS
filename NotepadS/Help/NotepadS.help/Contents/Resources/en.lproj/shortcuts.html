Keyboard shortcuts

The most useful shortcuts in NotepadS.

ShortcutAction
⌘NNew document (in a new tab)
⌘OOpen
⌘SSave
⌘PPrint (also to PDF)
⌘F, ⌘G, ⇧⌘GFind, find next, find previous
⌥⌘FFind and replace (find bar)
⇧⌥⌘FFind and replace with regular expressions
⇧⌘FFind in Files
⌘LGo to Line
⌘D, ⇧⌘KDuplicate line, delete line
⌥⌘↑, ⌥⌘↓Move line up, down
⌃⌘JJoin lines
⌃⌥↑, ⌃⌥↓Add a cursor above, below
Insert (PC keyboard)Overwrite mode on or off
⌃⌘WWrap Lines on or off
⌥⌘IShow Invisibles
⌃⌘MShow Minimap on or off
⌃⌘LShow Function List on or off
⌥⌘←, ⌥⌘→Fold, unfold the block at the caret
⇧⌥⌘←, ⇧⌥⌘→Fold all, unfold all
⌃⌘E, ⇧⌃⌘ESplit editor side by side, top and bottom (again: close the split)
⌘=, ⌘−, ⌘0Bigger, smaller, default font size
⌘,Settings
