Wrapping, invisible characters, Go to Line and indentation.

ToChoose
Wrap or unwrap long linesView › Wrap Lines (⌃⌘W)
Show spaces, tabs and line endingsView › Show Invisibles (⌥⌘I)
Jump to a lineEdit › Go to Line… (⌘L)
Make the text bigger or smallerView › Increase / Decrease Font Size (⌘=, ⌘−, ⌘0 for the default)

With Show Invisibles, spaces appear as ·, tabs as →, and line endings as ¬ (LF), ↵ (CRLF) or ← (CR).

When you press Return, the new line keeps the indentation of the line above. Whether Tab inserts a tab or spaces is set in Settings.

NotepadS never changes what you type: smart quotes, dashes, spelling correction and text completion are off.
